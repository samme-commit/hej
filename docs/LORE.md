# The Forgotten Road - Lore Document

## Overview

The Forgotten Road is a dark fantasy RPG about memory, identity, and the truth hidden beneath a forgotten world.

The player awakens on an unknown road with no memories of who they are or how they arrived there.

The world around them is filled with remnants of a past that nobody remembers.

Ancient ruins.
Forgotten weapons.
Silent guardians.
Fragments of lives that disappeared.

The central question:

**Why did the world forget?**

---

# The Great Forgetting

## The Event

Long ago, the world experienced an event known as:

**The Great Forgetting**

Humanity lost its memories.

Not just personal memories.

Entire civilizations forgot:

- Their history
- Their purpose
- Their relationships
- Their achievements
- Their mistakes

Cities remained standing, but nobody remembered who built them.

Weapons remained, but nobody remembered who wielded them.

People lived, but some forgot their own names.

---

## The Truth

The Great Forgetting was not a natural disaster.

It was a choice.

Someone deliberately erased the world's memories.

The reason remains unknown.

---

# The Hollow

## Description

The Hollow is the unknown force connected to the destruction of memory.

It does not destroy through physical violence.

It destroys meaning.

It causes things to become forgotten.

A kingdom can exist, but lose its identity.

A hero can save thousands, but become a stranger in history.

A person can live, but become nobody.

---

## Theories

Different characters believe different things about The Hollow.

### Theory 1: The Curse

The Hollow is a punishment for humanity's sins.

### Theory 2: The Enemy

The Hollow is a living force trying to erase existence.

### Theory 3: The Reflection

The Hollow is created from humanity itself.

A manifestation of fear, hatred, and regret.

---

# The Player

## Beginning

The player awakens on The Forgotten Road.

They remember nothing.

Not their name.
Not their past.
Not why they are there.

However, the world reacts strangely to them.

Certain objects feel familiar.

Certain places seem to recognize them.

---

## Hidden Truth

The player was connected to the events before The Great Forgetting.

They were not a random survivor.

They were involved.

The exact role is unknown.

Hero?

Villain?

Sacrifice?

The player must discover the truth.

---

# The Three Paths

The first choice represents the player's instinct and influences their journey.

---

# The Broken Sword

## Theme

Strength.
Conflict.
The past.

## Discovery

The player finds a broken sword.

When touching it, they experience fragments of a forgotten battle.

A battlefield.

A war.

A person holding the same sword.

Their hand.

---

## Gameplay Identity

The warrior path.

Benefits:

- Stronger combat abilities
- Weapon knowledge
- Higher damage potential

---

## Lore Connection

The sword remembers a truth about the player.

But it does not reveal everything.

Was the player fighting to save the world?

Or fighting against it?

---

# The Forgotten Stone

## Theme

Memory.
Knowledge.
Endurance.

## Discovery

The player touches an ancient stone containing forgotten memories.

A voice speaks.

Someone recognizes them.

---

## Gameplay Identity

The mystic path.

Benefits:

- More health
- Memory abilities
- Hidden information

---

## Lore Connection

The stone contains fragments of the truth.

It suggests that The Great Forgetting was a sacrifice, not a punishment.

---

# The Silent Road

## Theme

Discovery.
Exploration.
The unknown.

## Discovery

The player follows a road disappearing into the fog.

They see places that should not exist.

Lost cities.
Ancient towers.
Forgotten homes.

---

## Gameplay Identity

The explorer path.

Benefits:

- More locations discovered
- Hidden areas
- Alternative routes

---

## Lore Connection

The road does not lead somewhere new.

It leads somewhere forgotten.

# Items

Items are connected to memories.

An item is not valuable because of its material.

It is valuable because of what it remembers.

Examples:

## Broken Sword

A weapon that remembers a war.

## Nameless Ring

A ring that remembers its owner, but not their name.

## Forgotten Key

A key that opens a door nobody remembers building.

The game explores:

# End Goal

The player eventually discovers the truth behind The Great Forgetting.

Should the world remember?

Or was forgetting the only way to survive?

# Design Rule

Every new feature, area, item, NPC, and enemy should connect to the central theme:

**Memory is power. Memory is danger. Memory is truth.**
