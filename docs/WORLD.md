# The Forgotten Road - World Document


# World Overview

The world of The Forgotten Road is a dark fantasy world shaped by the disappearance of memory.

Civilizations still exist, but many have lost their purpose.

Ancient roads lead nowhere.

Ruins stand without builders.

People live among the remains of a history they cannot remember.


The world is divided into forgotten regions, each containing fragments of the truth behind The Great Forgetting.


---

# The Known Regions


## The Forgotten Road

### Description

The place where the player awakens.

A broken road surrounded by fog, dead trees, and abandoned structures.

The road appears ordinary at first, but it contains hidden echoes of the past.


### Secrets

- The road changes depending on who walks it
- Lost memories appear here
- The player has been here before


### Important Locations

- The Awakening Site
- The Broken Shrine
- The Silent Forest


---

# The Silent Forest

## Description

A forest where sounds disappear.

Travelers report hearing voices, but never seeing anyone.

Trees grow around ancient ruins and forgotten monuments.


## Lore

The forest is one of the places where memories remain strongest.

Some believe the trees absorbed the memories of those who disappeared.


## Gameplay

- Exploration focused
- Hidden paths
- Memory discoveries
- Rare items


---

# The Forgotten City

## Description

An abandoned city that appears empty.

However, every building looks recently used.

Tables have food.

Doors are unlocked.

Letters remain unfinished.


## Mystery

Nobody remembers leaving.


## Important Locations

- The Empty Palace
- The Old Library
- The Memory Archive


---

# The Ancient Kingdom

## Description

The civilization that existed before The Great Forgetting.

Almost everything known about it is incomplete.


## Theories

Some believe it was destroyed.

Others believe it intentionally disappeared.


## Connection To Player

The player has a connection to this kingdom.


## The Ashen Gate

The Ashen Gate stands in the buried heart of the Ancient Kingdom.

It is the threshold where the Keepers made their final choice during the
Great Forgetting. The Hollow Remnant still waits beyond it, sustained by the
meaning the world tried to hide.


---

# Factions


## The Keepers of Memory

### Description

A group dedicated to preserving forgotten knowledge.


### Beliefs

Memory is the foundation of identity.


### Conflict

Some believe restoring all memories would destroy the world.


---

## The Nameless

### Description

People who completely lost their identity.

They have no names, no history, and no past.


### Behavior

Some are peaceful.

Others are consumed by the emptiness left behind.


---

## The Hollowborn

### Description

Creatures affected by The Hollow.


### Characteristics

They are not simply monsters.

They are beings who have lost too much of themselves.

# World Rules


## Memory Has Power

Memories can:

- Reveal hidden locations
- Change reality
- Unlock abilities
- Affect characters


## Forgotten Things Are Not Gone

Something forgotten can still exist.


## The Past Is Dangerous

Learning the truth has consequences.
