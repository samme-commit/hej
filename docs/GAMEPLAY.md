# The Forgotten Road - Gameplay Document


# Core Gameplay Loop


Explore

↓

Discover Memories

↓

Make Choices

↓

Change The World

↓

Uncover The Truth



---

# Player Identity


The player does not choose a traditional class.

Instead, their identity is created through actions.


The game remembers:

- Choices
- Discoveries
- Items
- Memories
- Relationships


---

# Progression System


Progression is based on discovery.

The player grows by:

- Finding memories
- Learning forgotten abilities
- Recovering items
- Exploring new areas


---

# Stats


## Health

Represents physical survival.


## Strength

Determines combat power.


## Knowledge

Determines interaction with memories and mysteries.


## Awareness

Determines exploration abilities.


---

# Memory System


Memories are the main progression system.


A memory can:

- Unlock dialogue
- Reveal lore
- Change locations
- Unlock abilities


Example:


Memory:

"The Last Battle"


Effect:

Unlocks knowledge about the Broken Sword.


---

# Combat


Combat is simple but meaningful.


Possible actions:

- Attack
- Defend
- Use Item
- Use Memory Ability


Combat should focus on choices, not speed.

Combat actions currently include:

- Attack — direct damage, improved by Strength and equipped gear
- Guard — reduces the next enemy strike
- Memory Ability — a path-specific action
- Use Tonic — consumes a hotbar tonic and still costs a turn

The Warrior remembers the Last Battle, the Keeper creates a Memory Ward,
and the Explorer finds an opening.


---

# Choices


Choices are permanent.

The player should not always know the consequence.


Example:


Choice:

Take the sword.


Possible consequence:

Gain power.

Lose another opportunity.


---

# Exploration


Exploration reveals:

- Locations
- Memories
- Hidden paths
- Lore


The world should reward curiosity.


---

# Items


Items have history.

An item is not just a stat upgrade.


Example:


Broken Sword

Attack: +10

Memory:

"The blade remembers a battlefield."


## Equipment and Hotbar

Weapons and charms are equipped automatically when their story moment is
claimed. The Nameless Ring grants a small Resolve bonus to combat.

The hotbar contains consumables only. It unlocks after the first path choice,
and can be used outside combat with keys 1–4. During combat, items are used
through the combat action menu so every tonic costs a turn.


---

# Endgame


The player's final decision determines the fate of the world.


The player chooses:

- Restore memory
- Preserve forgetting
- Become the Keeper


## Replay and Revisit Rules

Story rewards cannot be collected repeatedly. Combat may be retried after a
defeat, but a defeated enemy cannot be farmed for Echoes. Completed locations
retain a way back to their local hub or can be left through the map.
